# SystemBio Journal Club

A discussion-led reading community for biology, health, medicine, and neuroscience, with an initial focus on research in Nature Portfolio journals.

We study what makes a scientific question important, what a paper contributes, and whether its evidence supports its conclusions. Time series is one cross-cutting collection within this broader scope.

**All repository content, discussion posts, comments, meeting notes, and contributions are in English.**

## Start here

- Browse the [paper catalog](CATALOG.md).
- Explore [Biology](topics/biology/README.md), [Health](topics/health/README.md), [General Medicine](topics/general-medicine/README.md), or [Neuroscience](topics/neuroscience/README.md).
- Explore the [Time Series collection](collections/time-series/README.md).
- Read the [reading guide](READING_GUIDE.md) and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Join the [discussion forum](https://github.com/systems-mechanobiology/journal-club/discussions) to recommend a paper, ask a question, or organize a journal club session.

## How the community works

| Place | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Paper Discussions](https://github.com/systems-mechanobiology/journal-club/discussions/categories/paper-discussions) | One enduring discussion per paper, identified by DOI |
| [Journal Club](https://github.com/systems-mechanobiology/journal-club/discussions/categories/journal-club) | Meeting agenda, paper links, and session notes |
| [Cross-Paper Insights](https://github.com/systems-mechanobiology/journal-club/discussions/categories/cross-paper-insights) | Comparisons, disagreements, and research questions spanning papers |
| [Questions & Help](https://github.com/systems-mechanobiology/journal-club/discussions/categories/questions-help) | Focused questions with an answer when appropriate |
| Issues | Specific follow-up work with an owner and completion criteria |
| Pull requests | Reviewed changes to the catalog, guides, and shared synthesis |

A participant can contribute by leaving one question or observation. A full review is not required. Paper conversations remain open after a meeting.

## Scope

Include selected papers with a substantive biological, health, medical, or neuroscience question, resource, method, or evaluation contribution. Systems biology provides an organizing perspective across molecular, cellular, organismal, and population scales.

Temporal data are welcome but are not an entry requirement. General forecasting, finance, traffic, weather, and unrelated AI papers are outside the initial scope unless a specific biomedical connection is established.

The initial collection focuses on original research in Nature and relevant Nature Portfolio journals. Reviews and perspectives may supply background when explicitly identified as such. Publication venue does not substitute for critical evaluation.

## Current collection

The initial catalog contains 12 screened reading candidates. Their citation metadata have been checked; the entries are starting points for discussion, not completed group reviews. Topic membership describes the scientific question, not the journal name.
