# Reading guide

Our central question is: **What makes this contribution important, and how does the evidence establish it?**

## Six questions for any paper

1. **Problem:** What was already known, what was missing, and who needed the missing answer?
2. **Contribution:** Is the advance a discovery, mechanism, method, resource, evaluation, prediction, or demonstrated practical benefit?
3. **Evidence:** Which two or three figures carry the argument? What do the controls, comparisons, and independent validations establish?
4. **Limits:** What alternative explanations remain? What does the paper not demonstrate?
5. **Journal fit:** Which readers would care, and why does the contribution fit this journal's scope?
6. **Transfer:** What question, study design, or evidence standard could we use in our own research?

Keep problem novelty, model novelty, resource value, and scientific discovery distinct. More data or a more complex model is not a sufficient explanation of importance.

## Topic-specific lenses

| Topic | Questions to prioritize |
| --- | --- |
| Biology | Does the study reveal a mechanism, interaction, cell state, or system-level principle? What distinguishes description from intervention-supported explanation? |
| Health | What population is represented? What biases affect participation, behavior, measurement, or follow-up? |
| General Medicine | Does the evidence establish association, prediction, clinical decision value, or improved outcomes? These are different claims. |
| Neuroscience | What connects measurement to neural computation or behavior? Which alternative spatial, temporal, and physiological explanations are tested? |

## Additional lens for the Time Series collection

- What is actually temporal: waveforms, repeated sampling, rhythms, state transitions, or modeled dynamics?
- Is this longitudinal observation, dense time-series measurement, or an inferred ordering? Do not conflate pseudotime with observed longitudinal trajectories.
- What would be lost by using static features or removing temporal order? Was that comparison tested?
- How many independent participants, animals, or systems were measured? Report these separately from cells, visits, windows, and recordings.
- Could overlapping windows, repeated participants, preprocessing, or future information leak across splits?
- Are claims tested across time, cohorts, sites, devices, or conditions?
- Does a prediction improve understanding or decisions? Do not infer causality or clinical utility from prediction alone.

## Assess publication value carefully

Treat journal fit as a reasoned interpretation, not a reconstruction of a private editorial decision. Where public peer-review records exist, link them and distinguish their stated concerns from our interpretation.

Nature emphasizes scientific importance and interest beyond a narrow specialty. Specialist journals serve their particular research communities; their scopes should be read individually.

- [Nature reviewer criteria](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-referees/policies-and-processes)
- [Nature Machine Intelligence scope](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/aims)
- [Nature Communications article criteria](https://www.nature.com/ncomms/submit/content-types)

## Record the reading depth

Use **Citation checked**, **Abstract screened**, **Full text read**, or **Full text and supplements read**. Record group discussion separately. Do not treat a seed catalog entry as a completed critical review.
