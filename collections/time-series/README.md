# Time Series

A cross-cutting collection within SystemBio Journal Club. The community's broader biological and biomedical scope also includes papers that do not use time-series methods.

This collection includes measured biological signals, longitudinal observations, repeated sampling, rhythms, neural dynamics, and methods for studying them. Dense waveforms, sparse repeated measurements, and inferred dynamics should be described separately.

## Discussion prompts

- What information is lost when temporal order or repeated sampling is removed?
- What is the independent unit of evidence?
- Are time, participant, site, or device shifts evaluated appropriately?
- Does the temporal component enable a scientific insight, useful measurement, or defensible prediction?

Follow the [reading guide](../../READING_GUIDE.md) and keep one discussion per DOI.
