## Reading context

The source registers cover approximately six million Danish residents; the filtered study dataset contains 3,252,086 people with histories from 2008–2015. The eligible mortality cohort and linked personality survey sample are different subsets, not independent national cohorts.

The method adapts established transformers to linked health and socioeconomic histories. [BEHRT (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-62922-y) and [Med-BERT (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-021-00455-y) provide relevant earlier health-record applications. The paper's comparisons support gains over the evaluated baselines, rather than superiority to every contemporary model.

The learned representations provide a way to examine life trajectories and predictability. Predictive patterns and model sensitivity do not establish causal mechanisms or clinical benefit.
